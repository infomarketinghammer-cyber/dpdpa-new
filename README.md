# DPDPA Privacy Compliance Suite

> Enterprise multi-tenant SaaS privacy management platform engineered for Indian organisations under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023** and draft procedural rules (2025/2026).

---

## 1. Product Overview & Architecture

The suite implements **11 individually licensable modular tools** plus a premium **CPO Oversight Dashboard**, all backed by a unified **Central Compliance Database (CCD)** with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) isolation and transactional outbox event delivery.

### 11 Modular Licensable Tools:
1. **Consent & Preference Manager (Section 6 & 9)**: Unbundled affirmative consent capture (zero pre-ticked opt-ins), immutable notice snapshots, cryptographic consent receipts, parental verification workflow for minors, and self-service withdrawal triggers.
2. **Data Discovery (Section 8(4))**: Electronic and physical personal data asset inventory, Indian data locality verification (`ap-south-1` Mumbai / `ap-south-2` Hyderabad), encryption posture auditing (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit), legacy data item remediation, and infrastructure scanner connector fixtures.
3. **Data Mapper (RoPA & Legal Basis Matrix)**: Record of Processing Activities adhering to DPDPA obligations, legal basis matrix (Section 6 explicit consent, Section 7 certain legitimate uses, statutory mandates), missing basis warning flags, linked CCD assets vs manual fallback references, and Approver sign-off gates.
4. **Data Flow Mapper (Section 16)**: Directed graph and tabular transfer topology modeling assets, teams, processors, and cloud destinations. Automatic cross-border transmission filtering and missing safeguard alerts.
5. **Data Principal Rights Manager (Sections 11, 12, 14)**: Mobile-friendly embeddable intake, reference tracking (`DSR-2026-XXXX`), statutory SLA countdowns (30 days for DSR, 7 days for Section 12 grievances), identity verification, Section 8(7) erasure retention exception & legal hold reviews, and response payload delivery.
6. **Vendor Risk Manager (Section 8(2))**: 5-stage onboarding workflow (Registration -> Due Diligence -> DPA Contract -> Legal Review -> Approval Gates), DPA expiration tracker with automated renewal task generation, and subprocessor registers.
7. **Privacy Policy & Notice Generator (Section 6(1))**: Versioned multilingual templates (English, Hindi, Marathi), CCTV video surveillance notice generator, content diff viewer between releases, and Gemini 3.8 Flash AI-assisted drafting with mandatory Approver gates.
8. **DPIA Wizard (Section 10(2)(c))**: 6-step statutory Data Protection Impact Assessment (Screening -> Context -> Necessity & Proportionality -> Risk Analysis -> Mitigations -> Sign-off), with automated deduplicated risk routing to the Risk Register.
9. **Risk Register**: 5x5 Likelihood x Impact matrix, inherent vs residual risk scoring, and stable business deduplication keys (`deduplication_key`) preventing duplicate entries across event retries.
10. **Compliance Automation (Section 8(7))**: Asynchronous task engine, automated cessation tasks generated by consent withdrawals, retention schedule manager, legal hold registry, and regulatory compliance calendar.
11. **Breach Manager (Section 8(6))**: Personal data incident intake, separate initial (6-hour) and detailed (72-hour) Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) statutory notifications, Data Principal notification tracking, and authoritative server-timestamp countdowns (marked "Product-owner review required").
+ **CPO Oversight Dashboard**: Premium workspace with published statutory weights (Discovery 10%, Data Mapper 15%, Flow Mapper 10%, Consent 10%, Policy 10%, Rights 10%, Vendor 15%, Risk 5%, Automation 10%, Breach 5%; DPIA tracked separately), sync lag alerts (>30s), active breach panel, multi-entity rollup, and **15-section Board Readiness PDF & JSON report generator**.

## 2. Environments

### A. Demo Preview (Active)
- Runs without third-party credentials with realistic synthetic Indian corporate entities:
  1. **BharatPay Financial Technologies Ltd.** (Significant Data Fiduciary, Full Suite licensed)
  2. **SwasthaCare Health Systems Pvt. Ltd.** (Hospital network & telemed, Full Suite licensed)
  3. **KisanTrade Direct Agri FPO Ltd.** (Limited license: **ONLY** Rights Manager licensed to verify standalone operation and manual fallback references)
- Persistent top banner: `"Demo · Synthetic Data · Indian Fiduciary Preview (Asia/Kolkata)"`.
- Real-time role switcher (Viewer, Editor, Approver, Admin, PPOC / CPO) with simulated MFA indicators.
- One-click **Reset Demo** button to restore initial synthetic fixtures.

### B. Configured Environment (Production Deployment Checks)
- Database: PostgreSQL 15+ with row-level security (`db-schema.sql`).
- Locality: AWS `ap-south-1` (Mumbai) or GCP `asia-south1`.
- Cryptography: AES-256-GCM Customer-Managed Key via Cloud KMS, TLS 1.3 with PFS.
- Outbox: Transactional event pipeline with idempotent consumers, retries, and SSE broadcaster.
